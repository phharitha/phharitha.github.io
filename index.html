A LITTLE ABOUT ME

From learning physics
to learning through experiments.

My journey in physics began with curiosity about how the physical world works. Over time, that curiosity gradually moved toward materials, experimental techniques and the connection between microscopic properties and practical applications.

During my postgraduate studies, I had the opportunity to work in a research laboratory on transition metal dichalcogenide-based materials and electrochemical energy storage. That experience became an important step in understanding the kind of research I would like to pursue.
